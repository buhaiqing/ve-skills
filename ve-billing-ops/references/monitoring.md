# Monitoring Billing

## Key Alerts

| Alert | Threshold | Severity |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| Budget exceeded 80% | Actual > 80% of budget | Warning |
| Budget exceeded 100% | Actual >= 100% of budget | Critical |
| Balance low | Balance < 30 days estimated cost | Warning |
| Cost spike | Month-over-month > 50% increase | Warning |
| RI underutilization | Utilization < 70% | Warning |
